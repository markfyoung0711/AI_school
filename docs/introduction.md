# Introduction to AI and Prompts

## What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to computer systems that can perform tasks that typically require human intelligence. Modern AI systems, particularly Large Language Models (LLMs), can understand and generate human-like text, answer questions, write code, and assist with various tasks.

## What Can AI Do?

AI, specifically language models like the one you're interacting with, can:

1. **Answer Questions**: Provide information on a wide range of topics
2. **Generate Content**: Write articles, stories, code, emails, and more
3. **Analyze and Summarize**: Process large amounts of text and extract key insights
4. **Problem Solving**: Help break down complex problems and suggest solutions
5. **Code Assistance**: Write, debug, and explain code in multiple programming languages
6. **Learning Support**: Explain concepts, provide examples, and guide learning
7. **Creative Tasks**: Brainstorm ideas, write creatively, and think through scenarios
8. **Translation**: Convert text between languages
9. **Data Processing**: Structure and organize information

## What is a Prompt?

A **prompt** is the input or instruction you give to an AI system. It's how you communicate what you want the AI to do. The quality and clarity of your prompt significantly affects the quality of the AI's response.

### Good Prompt Examples:

- **Specific**: "Explain the concept of reinforcement learning in simple terms"
- **Contextual**: "I'm a beginner programmer. Can you show me how to write a Python function that calculates factorial?"
- **Clear Goal**: "List 5 practical use cases for AI in education"

### Poor Prompt Examples:

- **Too Vague**: "Tell me about stuff"
- **Unclear Intent**: "Code"
- **Missing Context**: "How do I fix this?" (without providing what "this" is)

## Key Concepts

### Tokens
Tokens are the basic units of text that AI processes. A token can be a word, part of a word, or even punctuation. Understanding tokens helps you work within AI system limits.

### Context
Context is the information the AI has access to during a conversation. This includes previous messages in the conversation and any background information you provide.

### Iterative Planning
This is the process of refining your approach through multiple steps, learning from each interaction to improve results.

## How to Get Started

1. **Start Simple**: Begin with clear, straightforward questions
2. **Be Specific**: The more details you provide, the better the response
3. **Iterate**: If the first response isn't perfect, refine your prompt
4. **Experiment**: Try different approaches to see what works best
5. **Ask for Examples**: Request concrete examples to better understand concepts
