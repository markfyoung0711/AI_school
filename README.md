# AI School

AI School is a comprehensive learning resource designed to help you understand and effectively use AI systems. Whether you're curious about how AI works, want to learn about concepts like RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback), or simply want to explore what AI can do through prompts, you're in the right place!

## 📖 Learning Path

### Beginner Level
- Understand what AI is and how it works
- Learn to write clear, effective prompts
- Explore basic AI capabilities
- Practice with simple examples

### Intermediate Level
- Master prompt engineering techniques
- Understand AI limitations and how to work around them
- Learn about RLHF and how AI improves
- Apply AI to real-world tasks

### Advanced Level
- Develop sophisticated prompting strategies
- Understand AI architecture and training concepts
- Use iterative planning for complex tasks
- Explore edge cases and advanced capabilities

## 💡 Key Concepts

- **Prompts**: Instructions you give to AI systems
- **Contexts**: Information the AI has access to
- **Tokens**: Basic units of text AI processes
- **Iterative Planning**: Refining your approach through multiple steps
- **RLHF**: How AI learns from human feedback
